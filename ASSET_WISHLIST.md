# Asset wishlist

What the game would use next, roughly in order of how much each would add. Models as `.glb` (metres, +Y up, origin at the base centre, facing +Z), pictures as PNG, sounds as `.mp3` or `.m4a`.

## Sound (there is none yet)

1. Footsteps: soft creeping steps and louder running steps on wood (a few variations of each).
2. A glass clink for picking something up, and a brighter chime for the last one.
3. A watchman's "hm?" and "hey!" (wordless), and a whistle for the alarm.
4. A door creaking open; a chair creak for sitting down.
5. A quiet night ambience loop (clock ticking, wind, a far-off owl), and a tense loop for a chase.
6. A short win jingle and a "caught" sting.

## People

7. A watchman's hand lantern (a part for the right hand), and a peaked cap.
8. A burglar's eye mask and a striped jumper; a swag sack for the hand that fills up.
9. Animations: tiptoe (a creeping walk), crouch and crouch-walk, a startled jump, looking left and right, a "gotcha" grab, falling asleep in a chair and snoring.
10. A cat: sits, walks, and knocks things off tables.

## Things to take, and to hide in

11. Treasures other than bottles: a jewelled box, a gold watch, a painting to lift off the wall, a trophy, a fat ledger, a cash register drawer.
12. Hiding places: a wardrobe, a big laundry basket, a long curtain, a grandfather clock, a large cardboard box.
13. Things that make noise if bumped: a suit of armour, a stack of tins, a bead curtain, a squeaky floorboard tile.
14. A safe (to crack for a bonus), a key on a hook, and a locked display cabinet.

## Rooms

15. Interior walls: a half-height partition, a wall with an open archway, and a 1 m wall piece, so nights can have several rooms and corridors.
16. A window that can be climbed through (open and shut), as a second way in or out.
17. A staircase and a trapdoor, for moving between floors.
18. Floors: tile, carpet (quiet to run on) and creaky boards; a doormat.
19. Other places to rob with the same kit: a museum set (plinths, velvet ropes, glass cases), a bakery set and a library set.
20. A ceiling lamp and a light switch, a security camera, and a laser tripwire post.

## Interface

21. Icons: an eye (open, half, shut) for the alarm meter, a footprint for noise, a chair for "sit", a door, a moon for the night counter.
22. A title logo, and an app icon to replace the old one.
23. A chunky display font.
24. "?" and "!" bubbles to replace the ones drawn in code.
