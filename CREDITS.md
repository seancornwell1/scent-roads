## Made for this game

Nov 10, 2026: every model in this table was exported again in the warm palette (walnut and mahogany woods, cream plaster, burgundy, forest green, gold), coloured with painted vertex colours. Where a row written on Nov 9 names a pastel colour (mint, pink, lilac, sky, butter, sage), read the warm colour that replaced it in `blender/lib.py` `PAL`; shapes, sizes and triangle counts in those rows are unchanged unless the row says otherwise. Not placed in a room or offered as furniture at the moment: `wall-shelf`, `wall-shopfront`, `perfume-bottle-tall`, `perfume-bottle-atomizer`.
