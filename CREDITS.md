## Grab3D (CC0)

Grab3D ([grab3d.com](https://grab3d.com)) releases every model under [CC0 1.0](https://grab3d.com/license/); no attribution is required, but credit is given here anyway. Grab3D states that many models start as AI-generated drafts corrected in Blender, and that kits are assembled by scripts ([how they make models](https://grab3d.com/how-we-make-models/)).

**Changes made:** every model's textures were downscaled from 2048 px to 512 px with glTF Transform, so the shop fits in an iPhone's memory. Geometry and materials are unchanged.
